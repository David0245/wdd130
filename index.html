Ashaiman

Ashaiman is a bustling, multicultural urban town and the capital of the Ashaiman Municipal District in Ghana's Greater Accra Region, located just 4 km north of Tema. Founded in the 17th century by Nii Ashai, the town has grown into a diverse melting pot with a population of 208,060 as of the 2021 census. It serves as a major economic and cultural hub, driven by vibrant markets, trading, and youth culture, while bringing together ethnic groups from all across the country.
